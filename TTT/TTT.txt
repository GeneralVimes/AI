**Instructions for the laboratory work "Bots for the game of Tic-Tac-Toe"**

In this laboratory work, we will be building bots that play the game of "Tic-Tac-Toe".

**Step 1**
Open the TTT folder. Launch the index.html file in a browser and open the browser console.
At the start of the main.js script, a game world for "Tic-Tac-Toe" is created.

In the bots.js file, there is a bot for a human player, TTTHumanBot.
On its turn, the bot shows the game situation to the person and asks which cell to place a symbol in.

Play a game between two TTTHumanBot bots:
window.world.startTournamentBetweenBots([new TTTHumanBot("A"), new TTTHumanBot("B")],1,2,true)

**Step 2**
Among the bots, there is TTTRandomBot, which makes a move into a random empty cell.
Play a game between TTTHumanBot (which you will be playing as) and TTTRandomBot:
window.world.startTournamentBetweenBots([new TTTHumanBot("A"), new TTTRandomBot("RND")],1,2,true)
Record the game, marking the move numbers next to the placed symbols.

**Step 3**
Among the bots, there is TTTLearnerBot, which learns using Q-Learning methods.
Run 1000 games between it and TTTRandomBot:
window.world.startTournamentBetweenBots([new TTTLearnerBot("Learn"), new TTTRandomBot("RND")],1000,2,false)
Then, play against it as a human player:
window.world.startTournamentBetweenBots([new TTTHumanBot("A"), new TTTLearnerBot("Learn")],1,2,true)
Record the game, marking the move numbers next to the placed symbols.

**Step 4**
Examine the memory of the TTTLearnerBot:
TTTLearnerBot.memory

Consider a position where there are 2 X's and 1 O. For example, this one (if it exists in the bot's memory):

X| | 
-----
 |O| 
-----
 | |X
To do this, enter:
TTTLearnerBot.memory["X,_,_,_,O,_,_,_,X"]

If such a position is not in memory, investigate another one.

On a game board, mark the probabilities with which TTTLearnerBot will choose the next move from this position.

**Step 5**
Run another 100,000 training games between TTTRandomBot and TTTLearnerBot.
How will the content of the memory cell corresponding to the position change?

Can the TTTLearnerBot now play to a draw with a human?

How many games will this take?

**Step 6**
Implement your own bot for the game of Tic-Tac-Toe. We will conduct a tournament among all of your bots during one of the upcoming lectures.
